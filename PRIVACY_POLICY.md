# Privacy policy

The Bookmarker for Nextcloud browser extension ("Bookmarker for Nextcloud") lets you save and manage browser bookmarks on a Nextcloud server that you choose ("your server"). Bookmarker for Nextcloud does not operate any server of its own and does not send your data to the author or publisher.

## Information you provide

**Server address and credentials.** You enter the URL of your Nextcloud server and your login credentials (username/password or app password). These are stored locally in your browser (via the browser's extension storage) and are sent only to the server address you configured, to authenticate API requests.

**Bookmarks.** Bookmark titles, URLs, tags, and folder data are read from and written to your configured Nextcloud server in order to provide the extension's functionality. A local cache of bookmarks, folders, and keywords is kept in your browser to speed up the interface and is cleared when you clear the extension's data.

## Permissions

Bookmarker for Nextcloud requests browser permissions (such as `activeTab`, `scripting`, `notifications`, `contextMenus`, and `offscreen`) only to provide its core features — reading the active page's title/URL to bookmark it, showing notifications, and rendering bookmark folders. Access to a specific server's domain is requested only after you enter that server's address, and only for that domain.

## Information shared with others

Neither the author nor the publisher of Bookmarker for Nextcloud receives any of the data you enter into the extension. All data is exchanged directly between your browser and the Nextcloud server you configure. The author cannot make assurances about how that server, or its operator, handles your data.
